# Proposed features

Ideas only. None may change the behaviour of baseline features R1-R7
(see REQUIREMENTS.md). Each ships behind its own tab or menu, off the write path.

## Safety (highest value, no remote writes needed)
1. **Backup manager**: timestamped backups, notes, compare two backups, one-click restore.
2. **Change preview / diff**: show exactly what will change on the remote before any write.
3. **Config health check**: warn about unmapped buttons, activities pointing to missing devices.

## Convenience
4. **Activity templates**: reusable sequences (Watch TV, Play Movie) with device slots.
5. **Smart delays**: suggest delay lengths per device from the power/input steps used.
6. **Import/export**: share a device or activity as a file; import Flipper `.ir` (already present).
7. **Button-map printable cheat sheet** (PDF/PNG) of physical and touch-screen mapping.
8. **Search** across devices, buttons and activity steps.

## Remote extras
9. **Slide show manager**: crop/resize preview exactly as it will appear on the screen.
10. **Clock helper**: set from the PC clock and time zone in one click.
11. **Remote battery / firmware info** panel (read-only).
12. **Macro tester**: send one step through the remote's IR to test a device (needs verified write/send path).

## Platform
13. Dark theme, keyboard shortcuts, auto-update check (opt-in, off by default).
